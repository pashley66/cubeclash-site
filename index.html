Cube Clash Block Party Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

The short version: Cube Clash Block Party doesn't collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no chat, no tracking and no in-app purchases.

What information we collect

None. Cube Clash Block Party does not ask for names, email addresses, photos, location or any other personal information, and it does not use analytics, advertising or tracking services of any kind.

Game progress

Your clashers, trophies, currencies and settings are saved only on your device so you can carry on where you left off. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this progress. You can also reset it from the game's Settings screen.

Children's privacy

Cube Clash Block Party is made for kids and is designed to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children.

Internet and third parties

The game works fully offline. It contains no links to websites, no social features and no third-party code that collects data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. We will not start collecting personal information from children.
