Made by a dad and his son

Cube Clash Block Party is a father-and-son project by Peter and his son Matthew.

We love fast arena games, but we wanted one kids could play without ads, pop-ups, chat with strangers or pressure to spend money. So we made our own: dreaming up the clashers together, play-testing match after match, and tuning it until it felt just right.

Our goal is simple: a fun game kids can enjoy, and parents can trust.

Safe for kids, by design

Made for the App Store's Kids Category. Here's what that means for your family:

🚫 No adsNot now, not ever.
🛒 Nothing to buyNo in-app purchases. Every reward is earned by playing.
🔒 No data collectedNo accounts, no tracking, no analytics.
💬 No chatEvery opponent is a computer player.
✈️ Plays offlineNo internet needed. Progress stays on the device.
🔗 No outside linksNothing in the game leads out to the web.
